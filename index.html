About ChrisPa

Nature Refined.

ChrisPa Scents and Soaps LTD is a Ugandan business dedicated to eco-conscious wellness, aromatherapy and practical personal-care solutions.

Our work brings together handmade soaps, therapeutic candles, botanical salts, home-care products and wellness experiences. We also offer customised trainings, wellness talks and workshops that promote holistic wellness and sustainability.

Nourishing your senses, naturally!

Beyond personal careOur wider range includes eco-conscious cleaning agents, detergents, air fresheners, floor soaps, pet-friendly dog shampoos, laundry soaps, toilet cleaners, hygiene products and space cleaning services.
We welcome enquiries about products, bulk supply, business partnerships, training and wellness workshops.
